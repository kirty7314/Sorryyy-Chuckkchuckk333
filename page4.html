🐰 For My Sweet Bunnies 🐰
Melon & Musk
~ Her beloved companions ~
💕 🌸 ✨ 💕

Oh my precious little ones,

I don't even know where to begin... The silence without your soft fur, your tiny hops, and those adorable wiggly noses is almost unbearable. You two were more than just pets—you were her joy, her comfort, her everything.

"Melon, with your sweet personality and those cute little hops... Musk, with your feisty spirit and playful energy... Together, you were perfection. You were HER rabbits, and she loved you more than anything in this world."

Every moment with you was a treasure she didn't realize she was collecting until it was too late. The way you'd both come running when you heard her voice, how you'd cuddle close when she was sad, the soft thumping of your back legs when you were happy... those memories are forever etched in her heart.

She would watch you hop around for hours, finding happiness in your innocent little games. When she held you close, all her worries would fade away. You were her safe place, her reason to smile even on the darkest days.

"You taught her that love doesn't need words. A soft nuzzle, a tiny paw touch, or just hopping around happily was enough to fill her heart with so much warmth. She felt your love in every little gesture."

Sweet Melon and Musk, you brought so much light into her life. The way you ate your favorite treats, how you'd binky with pure joy, the funny little sounds you'd make... these moments are treasured forever. She saw innocence, purity, and unconditional love in your eyes.

I know you're in bunny heaven now, eating the best clover, enjoying endless sunshine, running through fields of the softest grass, and being as adorable as ever. But oh, how she misses you both so much. The world is a little less bright without your presence.

Every time she sees something that reminds her of you—a pair of floppy ears, a twitching nose, the color pink—her eyes fill with tears. Not tears of sadness alone, but tears of gratitude for having been blessed with your love for however long she had you.

"Thank you for all the love, all the cuddles, all the laughter, and all the happiness you brought into her life."

You will forever be loved, forever be remembered, and forever be in her heart. Sweet Melon and Musk, you were the best little bunnies anyone could ask for. You were HER bunnies, and she will carry your love with her always.

Forever your human, with endless love, happy tears, and a heart full of memories. 💕🐰

Rest in the softest fields of heaven, my sweet little ones. You will always be her angels. 🌸✨
